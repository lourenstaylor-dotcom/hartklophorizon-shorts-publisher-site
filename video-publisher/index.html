Video Publisher

Personal toolWindows

Video Publisher is a personal Windows application used by Lourens Taylor to upload full-length videos to his own YouTube channels and to schedule them to be published one per day. It is built for one person and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads a folder of finished videos with their thumbnails and text files for titles, descriptions and tags
  • Uploads each video to the chosen YouTube channel as a private video that YouTube publishes on its scheduled day
  • Adds the video to the chosen playlist and sets its language and category
  • Keeps track of what has been scheduled, so a later batch continues where the last one stopped
