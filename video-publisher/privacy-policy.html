Video Publisher · Hartklop Horizon

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-09Single-user personal tool

Video Publisher is a personal, single-user Windows application built for one person, Lourens Taylor, to upload and schedule his own full-length videos to his own YouTube channels (Hartklop Horizon, Bible Prayers And Stories and The Psychology Of Human Behaviour). It is not distributed, not multi-tenant and not offered as a service to anyone else. This page exists to satisfy Google's requirement that a connected app link to a privacy policy.

1 What the app handles

  • Video files, thumbnail images and text files (titles, descriptions, tags) that you choose from your own computer
  • The name, ID and handle of each YouTube channel you connect, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues for it
  • A list of the connected channel's own videos, read from the YouTube API (titles, whether each is public, private or scheduled, its publish time and its length), used to avoid uploading the same video twice and to plan publish dates
  • The names of your playlists, so you can choose where a video goes

1.1 Where it is stored

All of the above is stored locally, in a SQLite database in the application's data folder on the laptop it runs on. The sign-in tokens are encrypted with the Windows data protection service for your Windows account. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app's developer, because none exists: there is no backend, no hosting, no advertising and no analytics. Deleting the application's data folder (%APPDATA%\VideoPublisher) removes everything it stored.

1.2 Who it is shared with

The video, thumbnail, title, description, tags, language and playlist of each upload are sent directly from your laptop to YouTube, using the official YouTube Data API. That is the only sharing the app performs. Once YouTube receives that content, its handling is governed by Google's own policies, not this page.

No third parties, no ad networks, no analytics. The only network connections the app makes are to Google's sign-in and token services and to the YouTube Data API. Your web browser is opened only to show Google's own sign-in page.

2 Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the single permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube only to upload, schedule and organise videos on channels you own, and it does not use that data for advertising, does not sell it and does not allow humans to read it except with your own action in the app.

By using the app you are also using YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply.

3 Your control

You connect each channel through Google's own consent screen and can revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access, independent of the app. You can also remove a connected channel inside the app, and delete the app's data folder as described above.

4 Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information about anyone other than the owner of the connected channels.

5 Changes

Because this is a personal tool rather than a public product, this page is updated only when what the app actually does changes. The effective date above reflects the last such change.
